HOW TO PLAY

  • Steer: swipe on the board, tap the arrow buttons, or use arrow keys / WASD.
  • Eat & grow: every food pixel is +1 point and makes you longer.
  • Avoid: your own body, and the walls in Classic mode.
  • Wrap / Practice: leave one edge, return from the opposite one.
  • Pause: the II button or Space. Start / restart: buttons or Enter.
